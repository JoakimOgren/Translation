## Divine Preparation

Apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors and teachers are God’s way of fitting us for service. That’s what the Word says. So I need to come under the sway of people exercising these gifts. 
These offices are Christ’s victory gift to the church; the expression of his love and the climax of his triumph over the forces of evil. To proudly imagine we don’t need them is to slight our mighty Deliverer, spurn his loving gifts and insult his unapproachable wisdom.
