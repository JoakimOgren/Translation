## Innocent Victim

For centuries, Israel’s appropriation of the promised land was blocked. It was beyond their control: they had to wait until ‘the iniquity of the Amorites was full’.
That time finally arrived and Joshua was ready. But forty years limped by before he could begin Canaan’s conquest. His mission was mothballed because of the sins of his people.
And while he was waiting, he couldn’t even begin his vocation as leader of Israel. This, too, was outside his control. Moses was still alive and Israel needed only one leader.
So what was he doing during this time? Elisha, centuries later, was known as the man who poured water on Elijah’s hands. Joshua, too, might have been little more than servant to the man of God. No matter how valuable and potentially satisfying this service was, I suspect he sensed a niggling emptiness about it. He was marked for other things. But the time would come when all the pieces were divinely fitted. He could then triumphantly assume the role he was born for.
